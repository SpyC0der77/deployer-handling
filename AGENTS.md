Lingo:
- **Grip**: The deployer is holding onto a handle or object
- **Pull**: The deployer is pulling the handle toward itself
- **Hitch**: The deployer is hitching the handle to a target point
- **Latch**: The deployer latch locking onto a handle or anchor
- **Anchor**: The sublevel that the sublevel that is linked to it via a grip is pulled towards
- **Rider**: The sublevel that is pulled towards the anchor
